You — the player
Open the client app and want to play a hand.
🪪
Login / Register screen
Type your username and password.
🔐
Secret handshake
Client and server agree on a secret key so nobody can spy.
📦
Encrypted messages
Every message is locked in an envelope before it travels.
🖥️
Game server
Listens for players and runs the game.
🧵
One thread per player
A helper for each player so many can play at once.
💾
Profiles file
Saves your account, credits and win/loss record.
💰
Place your bet
Server checks you have enough credits, then locks the bet in.
🃏
Cards are dealt
Two cards each for players and dealer from a shuffled shoe.
🎯
Your turn
Pick Hit, Stand or Double. Server makes sure the rules are followed.
🤵
Dealer plays
Dealer flips the hidden card and draws until 17 or higher.
🏆
Win, lose or push
Server pays out, updates your record and saves it.
🔁
Next round!
After a short pause everyone bets again.
